RSI at 75.5 is elevated, yes, but in a strong trend, RSI can stay elevated for weeks while price continues grinding higher. The report even notes that SPY is in a “band riding” structure near the upper Bollinger Band. That is not automatically a sell signal; in a momentum market, riding the upper band is often exactly what strong assets do before a breakout leg. Treating the upper Bollinger Band near 738.70 as a hard ceiling would be too conservative.

That is full trend alignment: short-term, medium-term, and long-term. Conservative analysts may argue the market is stretched, but the more important fact is that every major moving average is below price and rising. That means dips are more likely to be bought than to become immediate trend reversals.

A conservative analyst might say MACD is “too high,” but that misses the point: high and rising momentum often precedes continuation, especially when volatility is moderating. ATR has cooled to 7.70 from above 10 earlier in April, meaning the market is advancing with less disorder. That is bullish behavior, not a reason to abandon exposure.

The 735–740 zone is resistance, yes, but resistance in a strong trend is also fuel. If SPY closes above 740, that could trigger trend-following demand, systematic buying, and sidelined capital chasing performance. Selling too aggressively here could mean giving up the upside expansion just as the market confirms strength.

The correct move is not to chase aggressively at 731–738, but it is also not to step aside. HOLD captures the best of both worlds. It keeps the core position alive if SPY breaks above 740, while allowing staged capital to enter either on confirmation above 740 or on a controlled pullback toward 723–724. A tighter stop would be overly cautious and could force an exit before the trend actually breaks. The 710 level also sits below the 10-day EMA area, meaning if SPY loses that zone decisively, then the short-term trend thesis deserves reassessment.

The strongest upside case is simple: SPY is consolidating just below a major breakout level while all major trend indicators remain bullish. If price clears 740 and holds, the risk/reward changes fast. Momentum buyers who waited for confirmation may be forced in, and those who sold early because RSI was high may have to chase back at worse prices. The conservative side is overvaluing the risk of a short-term pullback. The neutral side is undervaluing the opportunity cost of missing a breakout. The high-reward path is to stay exposed, manage the stop, and let the market prove whether this resistance zone becomes a launchpad.
